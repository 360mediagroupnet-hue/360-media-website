Weddings we love

A wedding is the one night everyone you love is in the same room, and we treat it that way. We run the music from the ceremony to the last song, keep the MC side flowing and set the 360 booth up where guests can't miss it. Your photographer and videographer can come from the same crew. Most of our weddings are across the Sunshine Coast hinterland and beaches, and we've also done engagement parties on the water at Marina Mooloolaba. You get one contact and one invoice, and you don't have to chase five suppliers in the week of your wedding.
