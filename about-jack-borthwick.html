From the boats to the booth

Before this I spent 1,765 days at sea. I hold a Master 5 ticket and spent years running crews on boats. That's where I learnt that a good crew and a clear plan make everything run smooth. I run every event and every client the same way.

What the photo booth taught me

I never planned on being in marketing. I bought a 360 photo booth, and it showed me something I didn't expect. People don't buy the booth, they buy the person behind it.

It also showed me what people want from their marketing. They want it fun, creative and a bit spontaneous, and they want it to actually work. Simple, elegant and on time. That's what we do now, for events and for businesses.

Why I do this

I love business. Give me five minutes with an owner and I'll be asking how it all started and where they want to take it. I've had thousands of those conversations, and most of those owners have been burned by someone who promised the world and didn't deliver. I've been burned plenty myself, so I know how that feels.

I can't sell you something I haven't tried. Everything we do, I test on my own business and my own money first. Then I tell you straight what will work for you and what won't.

I still DJ a lot of our events, and when you work with us you deal with me.

What I believe

Most good businesses don't need changing, they need showing. Marketing on its own isn't the answer, it's the ramp that gets you there. If I believe in what you do, it's my job to help people see it. I won't work with people or businesses I don't believe in. One crew, one invoice, no excuses.
