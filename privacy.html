Privacy policy

360 Media Group (Jack Borthwick, Bli Bli, Sunshine Coast QLD) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What we collect

  • When you send an enquiry: your name, phone, email, event date and type, your message, the page you sent it from and the site that referred you.
  • When you browse: anonymous usage data through Google Analytics, and advertising measurement through Google Ads and the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) pixel. These use cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, quote, book and run your event, and invoice you.
  • To keep a simple record of our clients and enquiries so we can follow up.
  • To understand which pages and ads work, so we can improve the site.

We don't sell your details. We don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who handles it for us

Enquiries are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms. We use Google (Gmail, Sheets, Analytics, Ads), Meta and Xero to run the business. Some of these services store data outside Australia.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Email hire360photobooth@gmail.com or call 0461 406 627.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on.
  • If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, tell us first and we'll fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
