What changes the price

Hours

Most weddings need 5 to 6 hours of DJ from the reception on. Ceremony to last song is closer to 10.

Standard or premium

A standard DJ suits most rooms. Big venues and outdoor parties need the premium full PA with subs.

One crew or five suppliers

Booking DJ, booth, photo and video together saves on setup fees and the back-and-forth of managing separate suppliers.

Location

Sunshine Coast and Brisbane are covered. Further out, a travel fee may apply.

Questions people ask

How much does a wedding DJ cost on the Sunshine Coast?

With 360 Media Group a standard DJ with sound and lighting is $330 an hour plus a $275 setup fee, so a 4-hour reception is $1,595 and a 5-hour reception is $1,925. A premium DJ with a full PA, subs and lasers is $495 an hour plus setup. An MC is $385 extra when booked with the DJ.

How much does a 360 photo booth cost to hire on the Sunshine Coast?

Our 360 photo booth is $205 an hour with a 3-hour minimum, so 3 hours is $615 and 4 hours is $820. An operator, props and instant sharing by QR code or text are included.

How much is a DJ and photo booth package?

The Reception package is $2,550 for a DJ with lighting plus the 360 photo booth for 4 hours, with setup included. It's our most booked package for weddings, formals and 18ths and 21sts.

How much does a wedding photographer and videographer cost on the Sunshine Coast?

A standard experienced shooter is $440 an hour and a premium shooter is $495 an hour, with editing always included. Photographer and videographer together for 6 hours, plus DJ and booth, is The Celebration package at $9,600.

What does a full wedding package cost?

Event packages run from $2,550 (DJ and 360 booth) to $4,400 (The Full Floor: 6 hours, MC and Dancing on a Cloud first dance). Wedding packages run from $4,000 (The Half Day) to $9,600 (The Celebration, adds photo and video), $12,650 (The Wedding Day, 8.5 hours) and $18,700 for The Full Day.

Is there a minimum booking or a deposit?

All prices include GST. Hourly services have a 3-hour minimum. A 50% deposit secures your date and the balance is due 7 days before the event. A travel fee may apply outside Brisbane and the Sunshine Coast.
