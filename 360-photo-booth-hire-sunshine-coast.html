Questions, answered

How much space does the 360 booth need?

Roughly 3m × 3m of clear, hard, flat ground with access to power. We'll confirm the spot with your venue beforehand.

How do guests get their videos?

Instantly — by QR code or text, on the night. You also get the full gallery afterwards, downloadable and shareable.

Is there a minimum booking?

Yes — 3 hours. Setup and testing is done before doors open, so the booth runs the full 3 hours for your guests.

What events do you do?

Weddings, school formals, 18ths and 21sts, corporate events, fashion shows and festivals. If people are gathering, we can cover it.
